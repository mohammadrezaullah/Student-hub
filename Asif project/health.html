YOUR WELL-BEING MATTERS

💚 Healthy Body, Happier Mind

Small everyday habits can support your energy and well-being.

Build Habits That Help You Thrive

Simple ideas for a more balanced student life.

😴 Quality Sleep

Keep a regular bedtime and give yourself enough time to rest.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Keep water nearby and drink regularly throughout the day.

🏃 Move Every Day

Try walking, stretching, cycling or another activity you enjoy.

🥗 Balanced Meals

Choose a variety of nourishing foods and eat regular meals when possible.

🧘 Manage Stress

Take breaks, breathe slowly and talk to someone you trust when needed.

📱 Digital Balance

Take screen breaks and make space for offline activities.

Progress Over Perfection ✨

Choose one small healthy habit to work on this week.
