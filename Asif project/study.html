Level Up Your Learning ✨

Simple strategies for effective studying.

🧠 Active Recall

Close your book and try to recall what you learned. Test yourself instead of only rereading.

⏳ Pomodoro Method

Try studying for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Adjust the timing to suit your needs.

📝 Smart Notes

Write short summaries, important definitions and examples in your own words.

🔁 Spaced Revision

Review important topics over several days instead of cramming everything at once.

🎯 Daily Goals

Choose two or three realistic learning goals before you start studying.

📵 Focus Time

Keep distractions away and create a comfortable space where you can concentrate.
